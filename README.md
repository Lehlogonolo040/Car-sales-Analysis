****** OVERVIEW OF THE CASE STUDY ******
.
. The Bright Motors project analyzes large-scale vehicle sales data from Dec 2014 to Jun 2015
. to uncover revenue trends, seller performance, market demand, and product profitability.

. The goal is to provide actionable insights that support inventory planning, pricing strategy, 
. and regional marketing decisions for improved revenue and profit margins.

****** DATA ANALYSIS AND INSIGHTS ******
.
. Data Collection and Cleaning - Raw vehicle sales data was imported into Databricks for 
. initial processing. Handled null values, inconsistent state names, and duplicate records 
. using SQL to ensure data quality and consistency.

. Analysis - SQL queries and Looker Studio were used to aggregate sales by model, brand, 
. state, mileage band, and month. Key metrics calculated include total revenue, profit margin, 
. average selling price, and sales volume by region and product tier.

. Insights - Ford F-150 and popular sedans/SUVs like Altima, Escape, and Camry drive the 
. highest revenue. Florida, California, Texas, and Pennsylvania are the top-performing states.

. Revenue peaked in February 2015, followed by a sharp decline in March. Low-mileage vehicles 
. (20k–100k miles) offer the best balance of price and volume.

. Recommendations - Prioritize stock for high-demand models and regions, increase focus on 
. low-mileage and luxury vehicles to improve margins, plan promotions around Q1 peak season, 
. and review pricing for high-mileage inventory to clear stock faster.

****** SUMMARY AND INSIGHTS **********
.
. Built a SQL-based analytics pipeline in Databricks to clean and transform raw sales data 
. for analysis and dashboarding.

. Developed KPI dashboards in Looker Studio to visualize revenue by state, top brands/models, 
. profit margins, and monthly sales trends.
.
. Identified inventory and pricing opportunities to shift sales mix toward higher-margin 
. vehicles and optimize regional performance.
